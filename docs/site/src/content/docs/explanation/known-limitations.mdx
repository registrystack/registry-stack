Registry Stack is pre-1.0 Beta software for self-hosted institutional pilots.

## Product maturity

- Interfaces may change before 1.0. Keep clients and deployments on compatible releases.
- Operators must supply production identity, networking, secret storage, monitoring, backup, and
  incident response.
- Generated checks establish contract consistency. They do not certify legal compliance,
  accessibility, source correctness, or institutional authority.

## Base Registry Engine

Base Registry Engine (BReg) has these current operational limits:

- BReg requires PostgreSQL and owns the records it stores.
- Model changes, data migration, backup, restoration, and retention require explicit operator plans.
- Authorization depends on correct identity-provider claims and reviewed access profiles.

## Evidence Gateway

- Evidence Gateway answers predefined requirements. It is not a general query or policy engine.
- A signed assertion proves what the configured service asserted, not that the authoritative source
  was correct.
- OID4VCI support has its own bounded profile and does not provide a general wallet.
- Source and assertion keys require operator-managed rotation and recovery.

## Registry Manifest and Discovery

- Manifest output is descriptive and static. It neither serves records nor authorizes access.
- Current Discovery runtime and clients support Evidence providers only.
- Published Discovery v1alpha1 profile resources remain historically broad. Operators must rebuild
  mixed pre-retirement indexes before serving them with a current runtime.

## Product removal

Registry Relay ended with version 0.38.0 and is absent from 0.39.0. No maintained component replaces
its generic SQLite publication, SDMX, or JSON-FG surfaces. See the
[retirement decision](../../decisions/registry-relay-retirement-2026-10-03/).
