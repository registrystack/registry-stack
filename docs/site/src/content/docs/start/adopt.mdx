Choose the parts that fit your institution's systems and team. Configure a Base Registry Engine
(BReg) project, add a small extension, write an application, or run a separate module. These paths
combine; none is a prerequisite for another.

## Choose an adoption path

| Path | Use it when | You own | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Configure a register | A starter's Records, Actions, and review policy fit | Model, access, deployment, data, and acceptance | [BReg quickstart](../breg-quickstart/) |
| Add a small extension | A documented rule, route, or page seam fits | Extension code and its decision | [BReg extension guidance](../../configure/breg/) |
| Build an application | You need your own journey or integration | Interface, credentials, correlation, and recovery | [Client API](../../reference/client-api/) |
| Run a module | The work needs an independent lifecycle | Service, storage, release, and policy | [Module conventions](../../configure/module-conventions/) |

## Build an application in your language

Use released clients or the public HTTP contracts. You own the journey, credentials, correlation,
and recovery; each selected product keeps its own authorization and effects. The
[access-token guide](../../configure/request-an-access-token/) covers issuer integration.

The starter collection and App Kit are private; request maintainer access for their instructions.
The [reviewed licensing starter guide](https://github.com/registrystack/breg-registry-starters/blob/main/docs/reviewed-licensing.md)
and App Kit's private `project/docs/customization.md` guide require that access. For a public first
run, use the [first registry tutorial](../../tutorials/first-breg/). An extension, application, or
module still needs authority the receiving product accepts. Casework review does not write a BReg
Record; BReg applies the reviewed Proposal under its own authority. Add Coordinator for a durable
sequence or Messaging for reviewed delivery only when the journey needs them.

A module may publish a static
[Manifest description](../../products/registry-manifest/reference/#adopter-owned-modules);
metadata neither enables nor certifies it.

## Choose what goes in the registry

Hold the facts your law makes you the source of and the decisions you are accountable for. Leave
payroll, documents, and generic workflow in their owning systems. Review rules, tables, formulas,
and deadlines with programme and legal colleagues; record an unstated legal basis as "not stated"
rather than guessing. [Modeling patterns](../../explanation/registry-modeling-patterns/) covers the
record and authority choices.

## Working with other institutions

Start from the partner and the situation, then pick the product. "Do not integrate" is always a
valid answer.

| Situation | What you do | Product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Others need a fact about your records | Publish a narrow answer, not the record | [Evidence](../../explanation/how-evidence-works/) |
| A partner needs to read or change your records | Publish a governed record API | [BReg](../../explanation/configuration-defined-registry/) |
| You need a fact held by someone else | Ask for the narrowest answer that does the job | [Evidence, as the consumer](../../tutorials/verify-an-assertion-as-a-consumer/) |
| You must load another system's data | Copy it in through a reviewed translator | [Adapter](https://github.com/registrystack/registry-stack/blob/e05a43a55ba5a9a178e088d6b8c686b0f89b659f/products/adapter/README.md) packs; its ingestion job is not yet released |
| A standard or national system fixes the wire contract | Serve it at the edge | Adapter facade profile |
| The person can carry the proof | Let them bring it as a credential | Evidence with [OID4VCI delivery](../../configure/evidence-oid4vci/) |
| Integration is not justified | Do not integrate, and write down why | None |

For each partner, record the legal basis, who defines the question, what crosses, the serving
product, and who owns changes. Write "not stated" for a gap. Promise Read first; promise Write only
when the source can refuse a stale update, retry a create safely, and return a version. See
`If-Match` and `Idempotency-Key` in the [BReg API](../../reference/breg-api/).

Point to records another office keeps; do not copy them. Store their identifier with its scheme,
and ask through Evidence when a decision needs the fact. In BReg an event record's subject or
source can hold an identifier another system owns, declared with `external: {namespace}`; see the
[event records guide](https://github.com/registrystack/registry-stack/blob/e05a43a55ba5a9a178e088d6b8c686b0f89b659f/products/breg/event-records.md).

Payment and treasury hand-offs belong in an application or module. The maintained product and
operation rosters define no payment product or payment-specific operation.

For how much to share, see [the disclosure ladder](../../explanation/data-minimization-and-purpose-limitation/#ask-for-the-weakest-answer-that-does-the-job).

Work that spans records, weeks, money, a legal clock, or agreement between parties is a process,
not only a register. Check [where each rule lives](../../explanation/where-each-rule-lives/) before
adding custom workflow.

## Choose the next step

Combine the smallest set of paths that meets the job. A configured register can have one custom
route and an independently deployed reminder worker. Add
[Coordinator](../coordinator/) only when a durable, reviewed sequence across waits is useful.
Before deployment, use the [operator handoff](../../operate/) to assign credentials, backups,
retention, and recovery to named operators.
