If people in your institution must answer or approve something before another system acts on it,
[Registry Casework](../../reference/glossary/#registry-casework) gives them that review and returns
their answer to the system that asked.

## What a producer gets

A [producer](../../reference/glossary/#producer) is the system that needs the answer. It sends a
[ReviewRequest](../../reference/glossary/#review-request) naming the subject and the policy to
review it under, and later reads one [ReviewResult](../../reference/glossary/#review-result): the
outcome, any public reason, and the exact subject and policy it answers. The result never names
the reviewer. A retry under the same idempotency key returns the first request, not a second.

## What you define

You write a [ReviewPolicy](../../reference/glossary/#review-policy) in YAML: the stages a request
passes through, which people decide each stage and how many must approve, the outcomes a
[Decision](../../reference/glossary/#decision) may record, what reviewers see, which producers may
ask, and the deadlines that apply. `caseworkctl` checks the policy offline and pins it to a digest
before any request uses it.

## What runs

`casework` is an HTTP service that keeps review state in PostgreSQL and verifies tokens from your
own OpenID Connect issuer. Reviewers claim and decide tasks through its API, so your application
supplies their screens. A request stays `reviewing` until a decision settles it as approved,
rejected, changes-requested or answered, or until its producer cancels it or supersedes it with a
replacement.

## What Casework refuses

Casework refuses an untrusted producer, a stale policy pin, an ineligible reviewer, and a
decision against a changed task, and it never writes to the source;
[How Casework works](../../explanation/how-casework-works/) explains each boundary.

## When it is not the right product

Casework is for human judgment. A check a program can make from data belongs in the producer or
in an [Evidence Gateway](../../reference/glossary/#evidence-gateway) assertion, and a change to a
registry stays with that registry, which decides whether to apply an approved review.

## Where to start

and takes one request to a decision in about 20 minutes.
