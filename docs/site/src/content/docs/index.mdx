Registry Stack is a set of services for institutions that hold authoritative records, or need to.
Each product does one job, runs on its own, and is optional. Pick the one that matches what you
need today; [how the products fit together](start/concepts/) describes the model they all share.

## Pick a product

| You need | Product | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| A signed answer to one question about a record, without handing over the record | Evidence Gateway | [Overview](start/evidence-quickstart/), then [your first assertion](tutorials/first-evidence-assertion/) |
| A registry you do not have yet: records, an API, permissions, and history, on PostgreSQL | Base Registry Engine (BReg) | [Overview](start/breg-quickstart/), then [your first registry](tutorials/first-breg/); later, [deploy it](operate/breg/) and [query it from an application](tutorials/query-breg-client/) |
| Authorized people to review a request before its sender continues | Registry Casework | [Overview](start/casework/), then [decide your first ReviewRequest](tutorials/first-casework/) |
| Bookable appointments over capacity you anchor, with short holds | Registry Scheduling | [Configuration reference](reference/scheduling-configuration/) and [API contract](reference/apis/registry-scheduling/) |
| A PDF from registry data that renders to the same bytes every time | Registry Render | [Overview](start/registry-render/), then [render your first document](tutorials/first-render-document/) |
| A curated index of providers that relying applications can search | Registry Discovery | [How the index works](explanation/discovery-as-an-index/), then [publish and consume an index](tutorials/publish-and-consume-discovery-index/) |
| Portable, standards-shaped metadata about what a registry exposes | Registry Manifest | [Reference](products/registry-manifest/) |

## Who does what

Every tutorial names the role it is written for. One institution often holds several.

- The assertion provider is the institution that answers questions about its records with signed facts through Evidence Gateway.
- The data publisher is the institution that maintains records through BReg or publishes metadata through Manifest.
- The consumer or verifier is the service that calls a product API and verifies what it receives.
- The operator is whoever installs, configures, and runs a deployment.
